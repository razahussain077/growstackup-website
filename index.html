Why specialists

One AI that tries to do everything sounds convenient. A specialist is easier to give a real job.

We design each employee around one responsibility, the context it needs and the boundary where another person should take over.

01 / OWNERSHIP

One responsibility

The employee knows what belongs to it — and what does not.

02 / RULES

Defined decisions

Business rules, thresholds and exceptions are part of the workflow.

03 / CONTEXT

Specific tools

Access is shaped around the job: CRM, files, email, phone, finance or internal systems.

04 / BOUNDARY

Clear human handoff

When judgment is required, the employee makes that boundary visible.

A living workforce
The interesting part happens between the jobs.

A good employee does not just finish its own task. It knows what changed, records it and passes the right work to whoever owns the next step.
