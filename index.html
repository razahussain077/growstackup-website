How we build one

We start with the work, not the model.

The useful part is not giving an AI a clever prompt. It is giving an employee the right context, rules, tools and boundary — then making the handoff obvious.

01 / JOB

Define the work.

Inputs, decisions, actions, edge cases and what “done” actually means.

02 / CONTEXT

Connect the systems.

Give the employee the files, data, rules and tools it needs to do the job.

03 / EXECUTION

Let it move.

Read, write, call, update, reconcile or route — with evidence behind the action.

04 / HANDOFF

Know the boundary.

When the work becomes a judgment call, the employee gets a human involved.

A few answers

The questions worth asking.

Is this just a chatbot with a name?

No. The design starts with a job: inputs, tools, rules, actions, records and a clear handoff.

Do we replace our software?

No. The goal is to work inside the systems you already rely on and automate the gaps between them.

Do we need all eight?

Not at all. Start with one job. Add another when there is another job worth owning.

Where do humans stay involved?

At approval points, sensitive conversations and exceptions where a person should make the call.
