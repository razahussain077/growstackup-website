The problem

Revenue can leak in the boring part of freight.

A delivered load is not the same thing as a clean billing workflow. Missing paperwork, mismatched rates and accessorials often create work that sits between operations and finance.

01 / INGEST

02 / REVIEW

Surface the exceptions

Turn issues into actions

You get a prioritized list of what to review, what evidence supports it and what should happen next.

What we check

FAQ

Keep the first step simple.

Do we need a new TMS?

No. The initial audit is designed around the data and documents you already have.

What should we send?

A small sample of recent freight invoices is the starting point. Rate confirmations and POD/BOL documents are useful where available.

Will this replace our billing team?

The first step is an audit, not a headcount decision. A later managed workflow can automate repetitive work while keeping people involved for exceptions.

What happens after the audit?

You receive a clear view of the workflow issues we found. We can then discuss whether a paid pilot makes economic sense for your operation.
