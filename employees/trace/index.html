How Trace works
Slow enough to notice the detail.

A packet arrives as six PDFs and an email. Trace reads each file, connects the identifiers, checks the required fields and leaves a clean record for the next employee. Four fields are missing; those become a focused request instead of another email thread.

Trace is designed around a defined responsibility.

Trace is methodical by design. Evidence first, assumptions last.
When Trace gets stuck

Anything incomplete, contradictory or sensitive is routed to the person who can resolve it.

The employee should not manufacture a confident answer just to keep the workflow moving. A good handoff records the state of the work, the evidence it used and the decision a human needs to make.
