How Scout works
Fast in research. Careful with assumptions.

A new account lands in the territory. Scout checks the company, maps the relevant people, compares the account against your rules and prepares an outreach brief. Nothing goes out until the workflow says it is ready.

Scout is designed around a defined responsibility.

Scout is curious before being confident. The job is not to send more messages; it is to know who is worth talking to and why.
When Scout gets stuck

When the conversation becomes strategic, Scout hands the account to a human seller.

The employee should not manufacture a confident answer just to keep the workflow moving. A good handoff records the state of the work, the evidence it used and the decision a human needs to make.
