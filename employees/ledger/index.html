How Ledger works
Calm under a messy spreadsheet.

An invoice lands against a set of source records. Ledger compares the fields, spots a mismatch and records exactly what does not line up. A human sees one clear exception instead of rechecking the whole file.

Ledger is designed around a defined responsibility.

Ledger does not trust a clean-looking number without a source behind it.
When Ledger gets stuck

Anything that needs a judgment call, dispute or approval goes to a human owner.

The employee should not manufacture a confident answer just to keep the workflow moving. A good handoff records the state of the work, the evidence it used and the decision a human needs to make.
