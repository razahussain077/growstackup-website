How Voice works
Short answers. Clear next steps.

A prospect calls with a basic question and asks for the next available meeting. Voice confirms the required details, checks the calendar, books the slot and writes the call summary back to the CRM.

Voice is designed around a defined responsibility.

Voice is direct, polite and hard to rattle.
When Voice gets stuck

Negotiation, complaints, sensitive situations and anything outside the playbook go to a human.

The employee should not manufacture a confident answer just to keep the workflow moving. A good handoff records the state of the work, the evidence it used and the decision a human needs to make.
